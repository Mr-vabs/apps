What this experiment can tell us
It can test whether adding state variables and state-dependent behaviour changes learning outcomes in this particular game. It cannot establish consciousness, subjective feelings, independent desires or biological survival instincts. The baseline is a comparison condition, not a claim that ordinary reinforcement learning is emotionless in every possible sense.
