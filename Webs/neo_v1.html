NEO begins with no learned policy. It explores three actions (up, stay, down), receives a positive reward for scoring and a negative reward for missing, then updates its Q-values.

What v1 is — and is not
v1 uses tabular Q-learning with a small state representation and persistent browser storage. It demonstrates adaptation through experience. It does not have feelings, consciousness, biological needs, or unrestricted self-modification. Those are separate research questions for later versions.
