60-second recap

Kahani short mein

Holi ke safar mein RAC ticket par train se ghar jaate waqt mera neela bag Gonda Junction par galti se doosri family ke samaan ke saath utar gaya. Bag mein do laptops the—ek company ka aur ek mera. Badhni pahunchkar bag missing mila. RailMadad ki ek helpful Didi, railway staff, Gonda ki FIR aur CCTV ke chakkar ke baad lagbhag do mahine mein bag wapas mila. Company ke liye FIR copy meri sabse badi priority thi. Aur haan—CCTV system chalane mein bhi aakhir mujhe hi haath lagana pada!

The full story

Ab shuru se suno…

01

Holi ki ghar-wapsi

Holi ka time tha. Ghar jaane ka excitement alag hi hota hai. Mera ticket RAC tha, lekin kisi tarah train mein chadh gaya.

Apni seat ke neeche jahan bag rakhte hain, wahan pehle se hi kisi aur ka samaan pada tha. Jagah nahi mili, to maine apna neela bag saamne wali seat ke neeche rakh diya.

Saamne wali seat par ek ladki aur unki dadi baithi thi. Unka samaan kaafi zyada tha, kyunki unki family alag-alag dibbon mein travel kar rahi thi. Dheere-dheere mera bag bhi unke samaan ke beech kahin chhup sa gaya.

Us neele bag mein do laptops the—ek company ka aur ek mera personal. Us waqt mujhe andaza bhi nahi tha ki kuch ghanton baad ye bag meri poori journey ka sabse bada topic banne wala hai.

02

Raat ke 3 baje, Gonda Junction

Raat ke kareeb 3 baje Gonda Junction aaya. Main gehri neend mein tha. Waise mujhe train mein aasani se neend nahi aati, lekin us din pata nahi kaise aankh lag gayi. Phone bhi paas mein kahin pada tha.

Usi waqt dadi aur unki family ka samaan utarne laga. Unmein se ek aadmi ne mera bag bhi kheench liya. Usne bina dhang se dekhe dadi aur ladki se poochha—

“Ye bag bhi utarna hai?”

Dono ne bag ko dekhe bina hi keh diya—“Haan.”

Bas. Ek sawaal, ek galat “haan”… aur mera neela bag Gonda par train se utar gaya. Na main jaaga, na kisi ne mujhe bataya.

03

Subah ka shock

Subah Badhni pahunchkar meri neend khuli. Pehle to khud par hi hairani hui—main train mein so kaise gaya? Mujhe to train mein neend aati hi nahi!

Phone mil gaya. Socha, Naugarh aane wala hai—bag check kar leta hoon. Badhni se Naugarh do station aage tha.

Bag dekha… to bag tha hi nahi.

Aas-paas poochha. Logon ne kaha, “Wahi log le gaye honge.” Tab upar wali berth se ek banda jhaank kar bola, “Aapka neela bag hai na? Wo le gaye hain.”

Maine poochha, “Jab dekha to toka kyun nahi?”

Uska jawab tha, “Mujhe confirm nahi tha.”

Us pal laga—train mein log dekh to lete hain, par bolne se pehle confirmation ka wait karte hain. Aur kabhi-kabhi tab tak bahut der ho chuki hoti hai.

04

RailMadad wali Didi

Maine RailMadad par call kiya. Ek didi ne call uthaya. Unki help main aaj bhi yaad rakhta hoon.

Badhni Nepal border ke paas hai aur wahan network kaafi weak tha. Call kat gaya, lekin unhone apne number se mujhe dobara call kiya. Unhone poori baat suni aur zaroori details note ki.

Maine poochha, “Abhi Gonda jaun ya ghar chala jaun? Mujhe pata hai bag Gonda par utara gaya hai.”

Unhone kaha, “Abhi wahan jaane ka fayda nahi. Tum ghar jao, aaram karo. Station pahunchkar mujhe call karna. Main Station Master se baat karke batati hoon.”

Ussi waqt mujhe yaad aaya ki mere fufa ke parents bhi railway mein rahe the. Unse baat ki. Unhone bhi ghar jaane aur agle din subah Gonda aane ki salah di.

05

FIR ka chakkar

Naugarh pahunchkar RailMadad wali Didi ne FIR karne ko kaha. Main station par FIR ke liye gaya, lekin wahan pehle mujhe idhar-udhar bheja jaane laga—kisi ne kaha yahan nahi, GRP ke paas jao; RPF wale nahi karte.

Maine Didi ko call kiya. Unhone staff se baat ki aur phir Station Master ne samjhaya ki unke yahan FIR ka process kyun nahi ho sakta. Unhone ek practical raasta bataya: ticket counter par jaakar unki baat karwa do, shayad wahan se madad mil jaaye.

Ticket counter par uncle ne Didi se baat ki. Maine seat number aur dadi-ladki ka description diya. Unhone available details check karke us family ka naam pata karne mein madad ki.

Iske baad main ghar chala gaya. Didi ko aur fufa ko saari details bata di. Didi ne kaha ki zaroorat padi to woh Gonda jaakar FIR mein madad karengi.

Agle din main fufa ke saath Gonda gaya aur FIR maine khud darj karwayi. CCTV bhi check karwaya. Footage se lag raha tha ki wahi log bag le gaye the, lekin bag common design ka tha aur footage poori tarah clear nahi thi. Maine apne paas ki saari details de di. Unhone kaha ki dekhte hain, aage batayenge.

06

Asli twist: mujhe FIR kyun chahiye thi?

Ab tak shayad lag raha hoga ki main bas apna bag aur dono laptops wapas paane ke liye itna sab kar raha tha. Lekin meri sabse badi priority kuch aur thi—FIR ki copy.

Company mein asset loss ka process tha. Company ke laptop ke liye FIR ki copy maangi jaati thi. Uske baad company apni taraf se check karti thi ki incident mein employee ki galti thi ya nahi. Agar meri negligence hoti, to mujhe laptop ka paisa bharna pad sakta tha. Agar incident meri galti ke bina hua tha, to zimmedari ka assessment alag hota.

Isliye mujhe FIR ki copy office mein submit karni thi. Laptop wapas milna achha hota, lekin us waqt official record banana mere liye bahut zaroori tha.

07

Do mahine baad: neela bag wapas

Main wapas Mumbai aa gaya aur beech-beech mein fufa aur station se updates leta raha. Kabhi unhone bulaya, lekin main Mumbai mein hone ki wajah se nahi aa paaya. Unhone kaha ki zaroorat ho to fufa ko bhej dena.

Ek-do mahine baad call aaya—“Bag ka koi photo hai? Kaisa dikhta tha?”

Maine bag ka description diya. Phir unhone mujhe ek photo bheji.

Photo mein wahi neela bag tha. Bilkul wahi.

Sabse hairani ki baat ye thi ki bag usi condition mein dikh raha tha, jaisi condition mein maine train mein rakha tha. Uski front zip locked nahi thi aur usmein mera resume rakha tha. Resume dekhkar koi mera naam ya contact detail nikaal sakta tha. Lekin bag khola gaya tha ya nahi, aur police ne use exactly kaise recover kiya—ye mujhe pakke taur par nahi pata. Bas jab photo aayi, bag bilkul waisa hi lag raha tha.

09

CCTV room ka technical support

Is poori kahani ka ek alag hi funny scene CCTV room mein hua.

Jab main footage check karne gaya, wahan baithi police madam ne kaha, “Nahi, aise nahi check kar sakte. Aisa possible hi nahi hai.”

Maine kaha, “Madam, aap rehne dijiye. Mujhe un sir ne bheja hai. Mujhe pata hai kaise check karna hai—mera kaam hi yahi hai.”

Phir system maine khud operate kiya aur footage check kar li.

Matlab bag ka pata lagane ke liye railway aur police ke chakkar alag, aur CCTV system chalane ke liye technical support bhi mujhe hi dena pada!

10

Wahi neela bag, wahi kahani

Aakhirkaar bag wapas aa gaya. Do laptops—ek company ka, ek personal—aur baaki samaan bhi. Do mahine ki calls, station ke chakkar, FIR, CCTV aur intezaar ke baad.

Sabse zyada sochne wali baat ab bhi wahi hai: agar us waqt kisi ne bag ko dhang se dekh liya hota, ya saamne baithe kisi ne ek baar mujhe jaga diya hota, to shayad ye kahani shuru hi nahi hoti.

Par hua ye ki ek neend, ek bina dekhe bola gaya “haan”, aur ek neela bag Gonda Junction pahunch gaya.

Moral nahi, bas yehi kahani hai —
Ek neend, ek galat “haan”, aur ek neela bag jo Gonda se Mumbai tak ghoom kar wapas aaya.
Ek neend. Ek galat “haan”. Ek neela bag.
Bas, itni si baat thi… aur kahani do mahine lambi ho gayi.
